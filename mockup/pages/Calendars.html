How sync works

Events show read-only in the week view, next to that day's tasks. If you choose a calendar under “Send deadlines”, each task deadline also lands there as an event.

Who connects what

Only managers and admins connect and sync calendars, including individual team members' calendars. Regular users can't connect or change anything, and they only see an event after a manager or admin assigns it to them in the week view.

Backend (later)

Google and Microsoft sign-in (OAuth), background sync on a schedule and a change webhook. Nothing on this screen is wired up yet.
